The argument is rooted in decades of psychoneuroimmunology research: chronic stress and emotional suppression have measurable effects on the immune and endocrine systems. Maté illustrates this through patient stories that are both deeply specific and utterly universal. You may not share the exact diagnosis, but you will likely recognize the pattern — the person who was always "fine," who took care of everyone else, who learned early that their emotions were inconvenient.

This book has particular resonance for clients navigating autoimmune conditions, fibromyalgia, chronic fatigue, and other illnesses that the medical system often fails to fully address. It's not a book that says illness is your fault — quite the opposite. It's a book that says the circumstances that shaped you had real physiological consequences, and understanding that is part of healing.
