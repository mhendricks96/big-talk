I have to admit, the plot line was more interesting to me than the actual book, and yet I definitely recommend it to a very specific audience. This book is for ages 30ish and under, the kind of young person who would absolutely tiktok/blog the end of the world. If that's not you, you may miss some of the creepy and yet satisfying moments of connection to the main character. Or less age dependent, you just need a healthy dose of delusion to cope. The world feels like it's ending every few weeks, and all I want do is make more spreadsheets about books and crafts!
