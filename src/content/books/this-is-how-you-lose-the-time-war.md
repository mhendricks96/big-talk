This book is a paradox in all forms. It's weird and not written like anything you've seen before but somehow, the love letter format back and forth between the main characters was a trope that I YEARN for. Something really playful and light, and yet extremely serious. I love when a cover so accurately portrays both content and process of the story. The ending was exactly what it needed to be, and yet i felt UGH. Unsatisfied? Worth it, for the yearning.
