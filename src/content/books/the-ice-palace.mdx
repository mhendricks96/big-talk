Grief!!! The metaphor for grief!! This tiny book is best read on winter solstice, but anytime in the darkness of winter will do. The writing style is unique, very aligned with the main characters development as a child which added to how grief can feel so confusing and dark and lonely, and like everyone around you is talking through a pillow. The girls that get it, get it.
