This book is tough to review without spoilers, because the themes explored are better left to smack you in the face. In this way, ignorance is maybe the theme that spoke most clearly to me, and I highly recommend leaning into your own as you start this book.

Things you should expect: lesbian spice (aka detailed smut) and yearning that makes Wuthering Heights look like a children’s play.
