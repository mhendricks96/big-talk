There were so many tropes and themes I loved about this book, which was impressive for something basically 250 pages. I love an adventure where the main gathers the team as she goes, I loved the Goblin Market and Bone Dog, and the chicken with a demon in him, the weird puppet lady, and a 30 year old hero. I loved the complicated ending of what it means, especially for women, to be free and make choices.
