Really great outlining of what practices are helpful for Indigenous people in the US. I really enjoyed the balance offered between historical and contemporary issues and finding the right “cocktail” of treatments. It also offers a more open way of evaluating anyone experiencing mental health crisis, especially the connections and explanations of unreality, psychosis, and dissociative symptoms.
