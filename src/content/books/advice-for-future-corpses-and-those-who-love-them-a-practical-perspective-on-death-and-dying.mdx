This book is for the crass, no nonsense children and loved ones of the dead. Don’t read if you don’t like swear words! The authors tone is MAD when people leave us in death, and how much of the process is paperwork and undesirable tasks. This book will outline for you what tasks you (or your parent) should do to make things easier on your loved one’s when you die.&#x20;

No one wants to find their dead dad’s playboy mags, so do yourself a favor and PLAN ahead, designate a friend who’s job it is to delete your internet history, and die knowing you won’t cause your kids more grief then they already will have.&#x20;
