I loved this book. The goo goo gah gah hot girl pink iconic energy is a brand I align with. The way she talks about the experience of blackness and race is approachable for white people, but for Black Girls first. Her footnotes read like girl talk side notes and tangents in the best way. I didn’t expect to feel so inspired, in the words of pop culture to be cringe is to be alive. I only wish I was younger now so that I had more time to embrace these lessons, or hear them earlier and more often. This is definitely for the girlies
