An incredible selection of essays about the experience of disability, what transformative justice could look like, and where we are in terms of supportive community. This book was published in Jan of 2020, right before we desperately relied on the care networks through the pandemic. Alice Wong notoriously calls disabled folks Oracles in that our experiences giving us foresight to predict the future. This is a book of Oracles, collectively calling us together and giving us the way forward when we needed it most.
