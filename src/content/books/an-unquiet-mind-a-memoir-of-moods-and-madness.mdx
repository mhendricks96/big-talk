A memoir about living with Bipolar disorder, this book is a super authentic and vivid explanation of what mania and depressive cycles feel like and look like to others. Her perspective is that of someone who “didnt need help” because she had academic and social success. I’d recommend this to anyone with Bipolar but specifically those who feel high achieving.
