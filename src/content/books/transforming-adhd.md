A decent exploration of what it means to experience ADHD and a helpful strengths focused framework to help you understand yourself, a loved one or a client (though it's presented as self help). It's a book for folks new to this content, so may not add information for anyone with a deep well of knowledge and experience already. It's a super quick read, and the content is approachable and an awesome start towards self understanding!
