A pretty great one month guide for restructuring your phone (and digital) usage. The first two weeks are preparation and reflections about attention, with only 1 week of experimenting with less phone, and a follow up week for reflecting on the process. I am generally less excited about fear mongering for social change, which was the entirety of part one but still found the process rewarding!
