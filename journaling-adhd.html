Journaling for ADHD

Event Description

Name: Journaling for ADHD: Counseling Group for Ages 12-14

Details: In-person group therapy in Beaverton (West Slope)

Date: Thursdays beginning in March

Time: 4:30-6pm

Location: Beaverton, OR

Cost: $60 per session, supplies included

Facilitated by: Alexandra Walker

Description: Using the science of creativity for neurodivergent healing, this group is designed for perfectionists, procrastinators and youth who are motivated to write, draw, and feel more organized. Each week, students will create their own “life planner” for academic, self care, home, and other priorities. By the end of the 8-weeks, students will have a journal of personalized supportive skills and increased confidence managing their ADHD.
